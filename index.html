Signals

Where a new design can still win

Everything below is derived from data already stored — publish dates, favourite counts, search rank — so it costs no extra page loads. Favourites stand in for sales: Etsy publishes no sold counts, and a favourite is the closest public sign of intent to buy.

Keyword Entry score Sample Median price Top 10% favourites Weak sellers New in top 30 Shop HHI
Entry score
0–100. How weak the sellers are, how often new listings break into the top 30, how spread out the shops are, and how much demand the winners show — combined. Higher means an easier market to enter.
New in top 30
Share of the first 30 results published in the last 90 days. This single number decides whether a new shop can rank at all.
Shop HHI
How concentrated the market is across shops. Near 0 means hundreds of small sellers; above 0.25 means a handful of shops own the results.
